# Business Rules

MemoFlo supports two categories of memos.

## Workflow Memo

Requires approvals.

Examples

- Procurement
- Budget
- Leave
- Recruitment
- Asset Purchase

Workflow is configurable.

Example

Requester

↓

Manager

↓

Finance

↓

CEO

↓

Finance

↓

Completed

---

## Direct Memo

No approval chain.

Sent directly to one or many recipients.

Recipients may

- Read
- Comment
- Reply
- Acknowledge

---

## Approval Actions

Every approval stage supports

- Approve
- Return
- Reject
- Cancel

Each action requires a comment.

Every action becomes part of the permanent audit history.
