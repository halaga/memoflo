# MemoFlo Notification System

Version 2.0

---

## Objective

Ensure users are informed of important activities without overwhelming them.

Notifications should be timely, relevant, and actionable.

---

## Notification Channels

### In-App

Primary notification channel.

Examples:

- New memo assigned
- Memo approved
- Memo rejected
- Memo returned
- Memo completed
- Comment added

---

### Email

Optional.

Examples:

- Approval request
- Memo completed
- Password reset
- Company invitations

---

### SMS

Optional.

Recommended only for:

- Urgent approvals
- Critical escalations

---

### Push Notifications

Future mobile application.
