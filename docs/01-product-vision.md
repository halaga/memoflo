# MemoFlo

## Vision

MemoFlo is an enterprise workflow and memo management platform designed to digitize internal organizational communication and approval processes.

Rather than being limited to procurement approvals, MemoFlo provides a configurable workflow engine that allows organizations to automate any approval process.

Examples include:

- Procurement Requests
- Leave Applications
- Budget Requests
- Recruitment Requests
- Asset Disposal
- IT Service Requests
- Internal Circulars
- Direct Staff Memos

The goal is to eliminate paper-based approvals while maintaining accountability, auditability and transparency.
